## A — Commercial architecture and spatial horror

Recommend 56 authored spaces: Arrival 8, Grid 10, Open Plan 11, Service 10, Blackout 9, Red Threshold 8. Credible commercial construction precedes continuity anomalies: acoustic ceiling modules about 0.61x1.22m, subtle square subdivisions, exposed grid, recessed lighting, plausible wall/baseboard/duct systems. Pale cream/beige walls, brown-grey carpet, dirty off-white ceilings; localized light creates yellow cast. Damp carpet darkens with restrained grazing sheen. Damage has causes (ceiling stain over floor patch, grille grime, removed-sign mark). Most rooms empty; rare landmark rooms 1–3 original props. Two stable landmarks per sector and useful loops. Tactical cover comes from architecture.

Small authored anomaly set occurs out of sight, unoccupied, combat suspended; preserve collision/current escape/objective access. Avoid frequently shifting maze. Originality watch: current Wikidot Level 0 title overlaps THRESHOLD and discusses blackout/red rooms; do not import its words/images/rules/narrative/entities.

## B — Pacing, objectives, agency and resources

Initial target 48-minute first run with 4–6 close encounters; figures require actual playtest tuning. Suggested timing: Arrival 0–5m, Grid 5–12m, Open Plan 12–22m, Service 22–32m, Blackout 32–40m, threshold 40–44m, return exit 44–49m. Relay order may permit Open Plan/Service variation if useful, without broadening scope. Circuit diagram at exit uses same destination/status language as physical relay panels. Each completed relay causes tangible light/machinery/shortcut change.

Every room contributes a route choice/sightline/acoustic identity/revelation/objective/resource/rest; count is not a substitute for contribution. Stable chase graph, learned loops and landmarks. Initial chases 20–45s, recovery 60–120s after genuine disengagement; pressure includes nearby searching. Readable attack windup and separation, first full-health hit survivable. Every mandatory obstacle/encounter remains solvable without ammo; guns purchase agency and retreat windows. Initial conservative resource hypothesis: 18 pistol rounds +4 shells, optional 3+1; revise based on hit accuracy and observed agency. No invisible bullet sponge lesson. Autosave relays/phase breaker and fair safe transitions; normally repeat <2m, never >4m after death. Do not implement empty waiting to hit playtime.

Proposed validation questions: objective understanding after Arrival, 35–60m first success, useful feedback during navigation, direction identification in stereo, explainable detection and alternatives, tension relief between peaks, meaningful weapon opportunities, no instant/lethal-checkpoint/resource softlocks. External human-panel numbers suggested by researcher are optional methods, not new mandatory contract scope.

## C — FPS action authority and validation

One weapon action state machine and one authoritative accepted ShotEvent drive ammo/hit/reaction/recoil/flash/impact/audio. Capture trigger/aim edge and resolve physics query next tick with no animation windup. CharacterBody3D at 60Hz; mouse render rotation separate from interpolated body, screen_relative input, never multiply displacement by delta; player aim independent of recoil recovery. Speeds 3.4/1.8/5.4, initial accel28/brake40m/s², normalized diagonals, feet-anchored crouch and standing clearance.

Starting pistol: 12-round magazine with explicitly documented chamber semantics, 0.24s fire interval, 1.55s tactical/2.05s empty reload, slide peak0.03s/return0.09s, last-shot lock. Shotgun: five tube +one chamber max six, 0.85s cycle, reload entry0.25/per-shell0.55/exit0.18s, pump0.24/eject0.38s. Shotgun motion/recoil materially stronger. Initial short stance blends60–100ms, one trigger buffer up to100ms, no delayed burst. Named idempotent ammo commit points and action generation IDs protect interrupted reloads. Menu/focus/death/load invalidate stale commands; require fresh edge after mouse capture.

Near-wall swept volume retracts with hysteresis, including lateral frames. Validate camera intended target AND physical muzzle path to prevent cover penetration. Aggregate shotgun hit reaction once per target. Surface profiles select dust/metal/carpet/organic impact, decals clipped to proper receiver. Finite pools for casings/decals/voices/particles. Pistol ejects with slide, shotgun during pump. Local dry weapon attack via AudioStreamPlayer synchronized layers; 3D tails/impacts remain positioned in world. Prewarm actual shader/material combinations to catch cold first-shot hitches. Motion scales down to zero; no default roll/FOV pulse.

Independent actual-play matrix (not run): clicks vs hold at30/60/144fps; full/tactical/empty cycles; interrupt every ammo commit with fire/switch/pause/damage; shotgun empty/chamber/load scenarios; walls/frames/cover; speeds/diagonal/crouch seams; all impacts and monster flinch/stagger; moving through tails; menu/focus/load; cold pipeline and zero-motion settings. Evidence includes build hash/hardware/input/caps plus temporal clips and ordinary encounter without debug assistance.
