# THRESHOLD game design

Pre-freeze; research B/C/D pending. Complete 35–60 minute survival-horror shooter, one seamless 45–70-space environment across Arrival, Grid, Open Plan, Service, Blackout and Red Threshold. Three physical relays restore unstable exit, then phase breaker and return. Original Listener with imperfect evidence and fair stalking, pistol and pump shotgun buy escape, ammunition tuned through play. No extra weapons/monsters/crafting/multiplayer.
