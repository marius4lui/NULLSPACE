# NULLSPACE quality gates

Status: initial gate register; freeze with GOAL_CONTRACT.md after research/design. This register operationalizes the entire contract and never replaces or weakens it. Unverified means NOT PASSED. No release scores have been assigned.

## Governance gates

- G00: Durable active goal, full original contract archived, supported model/configuration/capacity verified; root only orchestrates; implementation by Astra max workers in isolated worktrees.
- G01: All nine research roles A–I complete, cited, considered; game/art/technical design frozen with no scope inflation.
- G02: Required Godot 4.7.2 stable Forward+ and Blender 5.2.x LTS available, supporting tools and matching export templates verified. Any unavoidable technical contradiction is evidenced and resolved explicitly; no silent substitution.
- G03: Every significant feature has implementer build/run/interaction/visual/audio evidence, separate adversarial PASS and post-merge build/smoke/regression/actual launch inspection.

## Product and systems gates

- G04: Complete coherent 35–60 minute first-success campaign, six seamless sectors, approximately 45–70 meaningful intentional interconnected spaces and every minimum architectural variant; original Level-0 atmosphere and deliberately sparse props; no visible placeholder primitives.
- G05: Three physical remote relay objectives, environmental/route/AI situation changes and checkpoint after each, Red Threshold access, main phase breaker, return to exit and complete ending; no unmarked softlocks or resource-dependent unavoidable failure.
- G06: Responsive grounded player movement near 3.4/1.8/5.4 m/s with stamina, crouch, breathing/steps, flashlight, doors/interactions, pickups, damage/death/checkpoint and polished handling; usable configurable camera/mouse/FOV.
- G07: Both original polished production weapons implement EVERY respective contract animation/mechanical/reload/interrupt/shot/impact/audio requirement. Coordinated immediate fire; sensible wall/sprint handling; pistol/shotgun distinctly readable; gunshots affect AI and bullets produce behavior/animation reactions; ordinary combat buys escape, special final neutralization allowed.
- G08: Original 2.2–2.4 m Listener mesh/UV/materials/rig/collision and all 17 minimum animations, blended without obvious snapping; unsettling silhouette and coherent movement. No copied creature design.
- G09: Layered navigation, perception, belief/memory, planner and separate pacing director. Distance/FOV/LOS/posture/motion/light vision, room/portal sound with door attenuation and uncertain aging evidence. Fair stalk/observe/intercept/chase/search/retreat/recover behavior, eventual search decay, no hidden player tracking or unfair teleports/attacks.
- G10: All ten contract AI scenarios run repeatedly with evidence: wall occlusion; adjacent gunshot investigation; fire then relocate; nearby sprint; distant crouch; break LOS; change route during search; closed door; gunshot during stalk; stagger/navigation recovery. Actual gameplay corroborates tests.
- G11: Tactical doors affect sight, sound and navigation and permit distinct monster interactions; no permanent door exploit. Safe rare spatial changes occur outside sight, without popping/overlap/nav failure.
- G12: Deliberate quiet/recovery/rare sightings; no constant high tension or cheap repeated jumpscares; meaningful uninterrupted horror reviews address boredom, navigation communication, resource balance, monster overexposure and unfairness.
- G13: Original spatial synthesized audio on all eight required buses, layered distinct weapons/mechanics/tails, directional creature/footsteps/HVAC/ballast variation and meaningful blackout absence. Sources, processing and provenance retained.
- G14: Production-quality original architecture/materials/PBR/UVs/lighting/fixtures/props/creature/weapons/VFX survive close inspection: no missing textures, stretched UVs, seams, light leaks, z-fighting, floating assets or obvious artifacts. Restrained physical palette/postprocessing, readable normal sectors, performant fixture states and room visibility.
- G15: Complete title/New Game/Continue/Settings/Controls/Accessibility/Credits/Quit, pause/death/ending flow and minimal HUD. All listed resolution/display/VSync/quality/audio/mouse/invert/FOV/bob/shake/subtitles/dot/flash-reduction settings persist.
- G16: Checkpoints restore relay/environment progress, health/ammo/ownership and fair monster reset; Continue and restart reliable. Save corruption/loss, objective failure paths and settings failure paths covered.
- G17: Four quality presets; 1080p 60 FPS high target substantially met near RTX 2060/RX 6600 and six-core CPU. Report actual hardware, CPU/GPU frame times, worst-case traversal/combat/lighting, memory and spikes. Never misrepresent integrated-GPU results as discrete-target verification.
- G18: Telemetry includes duration, deaths/damage/shots/ammo collected+left, sightings/chases/durations/searches/false investigations/encounter intervals/objective times/frame time/errors. Tune based on recorded playtests.

## Visual and experiential release gates

- V01: Open full-resolution captures of classic corridor, open plan, service, blackout, red threshold, close weapon, medium-distance monster, active combat, breaker and exit. Every composition/lighting/material/cohesion/atmosphere/readability/originality/artifact-absence category >=8 during milestones, >=9 final. Anything below threshold mandates fixes.
- V02: Independent FPS play review passes input/shot/recoil/reload/sound/impact/transitions/shells/clipping/ADS-if-present/sprint/near-wall/hit reactions; prototype gun feel is FAIL.
- V03: Independent release review scores GAMEPLAY, GUN FEEL, MONSTER AI, HORROR/TENSION, LEVEL DESIGN, ART, ANIMATION, LIGHTING, AUDIO, UI/UX, PERFORMANCE, STABILITY, COHESION. Each >=9.0, arithmetic mean >=9.2, every score tied to concrete evidence. No circular self-grading or fabricated impressions.
- V04: Two separate complete final successful playthroughs from release exports AFTER last gameplay-affecting change; one conducted/reviewed by non-implementer QA. Record actual duration, deaths, defects, objective events, end state, commit/build checksum and recordings. Partial routes and tests do not count.

## Release gates

- R01: Zero P0 and P1; strongly target zero known P2. Open issues and limitations honest. No crashes/hardlocks/save corruption/unfinishable campaign/major broken systems/consistent cheating/softlocks/severe perf collapse.
- R02: Windows x86-64 export is mandatory. Linux x86-64 export is also required when the build environment supports it. Release launch and all menu/campaign/AI/weapons/doors/save/settings behaviors pass; no debug spam/visualization. Cross-export success or Wine execution must not be represented as native Windows runtime validation.
- R03: Fresh checkout reproduces build via documented pinned software/content steps. Record exact clean commit, commands, tool hashes, checksums and launch results. Runtime needs no development tooling or external asset access.
- R04: README premise/controls/requirements/launch/build/limitations, architecture/design/art docs, credits/license inventory/release notes, builds, final representative screenshots, final QA and 60–120 second honest representative gameplay video if environment permits.
- R05: Root independently rereads frozen contract/gates, issues/commits, rebuilds and launches release, inspects gameplay/screenshots/video and all QA evidence, verifies post-final-change dual runs. Any uncertainty blocks completion and creates verification/fix task.

## Severity and evidence integrity

P0: cannot launch, save corruption, cannot finish, crash, hard lock. P1: major broken system, regular AI cheating, major visual corruption, objective softlock, unusable weapon, broken checkpoint, severe performance collapse. P2: noticeable quality/animation/encounter/AI/seam/audio defect. P3: small polish. Tests may never be disabled to pass; no score accepted without inspection. Only all gates and the full contract together permit goal completion.
